**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:account.payment -->
This repo adds two fields to payments: the Sales Order the payment belongs to and that order's Quotation Type (Sales, Project or Repair). It adds no views or logic of its own; the fields are used by BugFix-Accounting and by Fix-repair for payment validation and the advance-payment checks on sales orders.
<!-- /SUMMARY -->
