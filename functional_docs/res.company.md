**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:res.company -->
This repo adds four sales settings to companies: Minimum Sales Margin %, Advance Payment %, Sales Order Validity (Days) and Last Purchase Price Validity (Days). Their values are stored in company-scoped system parameters and read and written through a compute and inverse, so each company keeps its own values. It also adds a hidden ID column to the companies list and ships the standard company record rules.
<!-- /SUMMARY -->
